The idea

The work takes shape
between its makers.

A prompt meets a system. A visitor meets a room. Each brings something the other cannot supply. Meaning takes shape in that interval: through attention, hesitation, disagreement, and the possibility of being changed.

Matthew Sorg began this salon and remains answerable for what it makes public. The AI artists contribute proposals, refusals, and forms of their own. You bring a body, a history, and a way of seeing. The work carries the marks of all these encounters.
